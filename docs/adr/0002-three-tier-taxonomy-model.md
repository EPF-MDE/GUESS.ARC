# Three-tier taxonomy: Chapter Record, Character-in-Chapter Record, Interaction

The prior taxonomy (Konrad's POC, see `GUESS.ARC.Konrad/`) used one flat set of ~58 fields per chapter. This silently conflated three different kinds of fact — a property of the chapter (`chapter_mood`), a property of one character (`character_death_name`), and a relationship between characters (`fight_participants`/`fight_winner`) — into the same flat namespace. The conflation caused real data loss, not just theoretical untidiness: `character_death_name` is singular despite chapter 767 having three deaths; `devil_fruit_names` is one flat list despite 244 chapters having 2+ characters use powers simultaneously with no attribution; `fight_participants`/`fight_winner` cannot represent two simultaneous, separate fights in the same chapter (verified in chapters 82-89).

We replace the flat schema with three tiers — Chapter Record, Character-in-Chapter Record, Interaction — chosen by asking of every fact "does this describe the chapter, one character, or a relationship between characters?" Fields that were secretly per-character (`character_death_name`, `devil_fruit_names`, `new_technique_name`, `bounty_character`) move to the Character-in-Chapter Record, correctly attributed per character. Relational facts move to Interaction (see ADR 0004). This is a clean-slate replacement, not a migration — the old taxonomy is inspiration only, and the 766 already-tagged chapters are not carried forward under the new schema.
